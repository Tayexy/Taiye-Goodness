GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before we start?

Here are a few things clients often want to know.

How much does a website cost?+

Pricing depends on the number of pages, features, content and integrations. Share what you need and I’ll help define a suitable scope and quote before work starts.

How long will my website take?+

Timing depends on project size and how quickly content and feedback are provided. I’ll give you a realistic timeline after we discuss the requirements.

Can you update a website I already have?+

Yes. Tell me the platform, what is not working and what you want to improve. I’ll review the scope and confirm what can be done.

Will the website work on mobile phones?+

Responsive layouts are part of my approach, so the experience is designed to adapt to phones, tablets and desktop screens.
